# Heritage Short

A 60–90 s short film built around an Amharic saying, and the code pipeline that regenerates it from one storyboard.

## Story

**Saying**:
The traditional Amharic proverb the whole film is built around and pays off.
_Avoid_: Proverb, quote, moral

**Composite story**:
A fictional story assembled from versions of the same situation the author has heard from different people; it depicts no real person.
_Avoid_: Real story, true story, biography

**Father**:
The elderly man at the centre of the composite story; the "gold" the saying refers to.
_Avoid_: Dad, old man, grandfather

**Son**:
The Father's busy adult son, who keeps postponing time with him; the one who counts gold as copper.
_Avoid_: Protagonist, child, boy

**Coffee ceremony**:
The Father's traditional morning ritual of preparing buna in a jebena and sharing it with stories; the film's recurring setting.
_Avoid_: Breakfast, coffee time, buna (in English text)

## Film structure

**Storyboard**:
The ordered plan of the whole film: every shot, its narration, and the music cues. The single source of truth all stages work from.
_Avoid_: Script, shot list, plan

**Shot**:
One continuous piece of picture in the film, with its own duration, keyframe and narration line.
_Avoid_: Scene, clip, segment

**Keyframe**:
The single still image that defines what a shot looks like.
_Avoid_: Frame, still, image

**Character sheet**:
The reference images of the Father, the Son and the coffee corner, generated before any keyframe and used to keep them consistent.
_Avoid_: Reference sheet, model sheet, style guide

**Moving shot**:
A shot whose motion is generated by a video model from its keyframe.
_Avoid_: Clip, animated shot, video shot

**Pan shot**:
A shot whose motion is a slow camera pan and zoom across its still keyframe.
_Avoid_: Ken Burns, still shot, static shot

**Act**:
One of the four story movements (Mornings, The fall, After, Two cups) that group shots.
_Avoid_: Scene, chapter, part

**Narrator**:
The older Son looking back, the single voice speaking all narration in Amharic.
_Avoid_: Voiceover, speaker

**Saying card**:
The title card showing the saying in Amharic script with its English translation, just before the end card.
_Avoid_: Title, quote card

**End card**:
The final card stating the film was made with AI.
_Avoid_: Credits, outro

**Gold grade** / **Copper grade**:
The two colour moods of the film: warm gold for moments valued, dull copper and grey-green for moments taken for granted.
_Avoid_: Warm filter, cold filter, LUT

**Radio cut**:
The narration and placeholder music laid over a black screen, used to test the story before any pictures exist.
_Avoid_: Audio draft, animatic

**Rough cut**:
A full-length edit with a picture for every shot and the chosen score, before final polish.
_Avoid_: Draft, preview
